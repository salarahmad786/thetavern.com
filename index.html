Committed to Reinvesting in Community

Stronger Jobs
Start Here.

Behind the counter, there's more than just coffee brewing. Our one-year youth job training program equips young adults in the Buckeye-Shaker neighborhood with the tools to thrive—on the job and in life. From learning customer service and leadership to building confidence and purpose, every shift is a step toward something bigger.

We're brewing more than coffee—we're brewing confidence, purpose, and life skills.
