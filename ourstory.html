Our Story

About Us Our Mission Our Purpose Our Vision Our Values Pay It Forward

Fueled by a Dream to Revitalize Buckeye

The Tavern Coffeehouse is more than just a place to grab your favorite cup of coffee — it's a story of transformation. What once was an old bar in Buckeye (hence the name Tavern) has been repurposed into a non profit coffee shop and a thriving community hub through the vision and support of The Meeting Place Church.

The name "Tavern" is a nod to the building's roots, but everything inside speaks to renewal, purpose, and people.

Our Mission

Brewing Hope in the heart of Buckeye

The Tavern Coffeehouse exists to bring hope back to Buckeye through strong coffee, stronger jobs, to build the strongest community. We're here to brew change — starting with quality coffee and extending into meaningful work opportunities.

Our space is designed to be a launchpad for local growth, a gathering spot for connection, and a symbol of what's possible when hope is reignited in a neighborhood.

13-min
Our Purpose

To Show the World the Power of Buckeye

We believe in the power of what can happen when people come together. Our purpose is to demonstrate that Buckeye is more than a dot on a map — it's a place filled with resilience, creativity, and people who care deeply for one another.

Through every cup of coffee, every warm conversation, and every job created, we're showing what community really means.

Our Vision

To see Buckeye as the centerpiece of new people, pouring into a thriving community.

We envision a future where Buckeye becomes a destination — not just for good coffee, but for good people doing great things. A place where newcomers feel welcomed, and long-time residents feel proud.

A city known for its momentum, its generosity, and its heart.

Our Values

Community

We were built for connection. From the baristas behind the counter to the regulars who sit and share life at our tables, community is the foundation of everything we do. We aim to create a space where everyone feels seen, heard, and valued.

Renewal

Taking what was once forgotten and bringing it back to life with dynamic purpose. Just like our building evolved from an old bar into a thriving coffeehouse, we believe in structural renewal, individual second chances, and fresh beginnings for the entire Buckeye neighborhood.

Determination

We are relentlessly committed to fostering strong local jobs and sustainable community transformation. Challenges do not stop us; instead, they fuel our drive to keep pressing forward, creating options where there were none, and modeling true structural resilience.

Generosity

Pouring into others is at the core of our nonprofit framework. Through our pay-it-forward initiatives, open-door hospitality, and continuous reinvestments into Buckeye, we believe that an open heart and shared resources are what turn a routine city space into a real home.
