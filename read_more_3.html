What Your Go-To Coffee Order Says About You

By Tavern Coffeehouse | COFFEE CULTURE

Because your favorite cup might know you better than your best friend.

Ever thought your coffee order is more than just a drink—it's a whole vibe? At The Tavern Coffee House, we believe your cup of brew tells a story about who you are, how you hustle, and where you find joy. Here's what your favorite says about you, with a few menu shortcuts to sip on.

Cold Brew - The Hustler

You're bold, driven, and have no time for fluff. Early meetings? You're already three steps ahead. That caffeine jolt is your fuel for big ideas, rapid-fire decisions, and getting stuff done. Cold brew people don't just survive the week—they conquer it.

Try This: Our smooth, rich Cold Brew is your hustle buddy. Order it here.

Vanilla Latte - The Optimist

You're the sunbeam on a cloudy day. Your friends know they can count on you for a pep talk, a thoughtful note, or a spontaneous coffee date. Your vibe is as warm as the steamed milk you love so much. Vanilla latte lovers bring calm to the chaos and kindness to the counter.

Your Vibe: Sweet, dependable, and effortlessly charming.

Chai Latte - The Creative Soul

A little spicy, a little sweet, and totally unique. You don't just think outside the box—you turned the box into a flower vase. Whether you're sketching, journaling, or mood-boarding your next big idea, chai is your muse in a mug.

Try pairing with: A Humry Bumry's cinnamon roll for peak cozy vibes.

Espresso - The No-Nonsense Type

No cream, no sugar, no time for small talk. You're focused, fierce, and probably up before sunrise. Espresso drinkers keep things efficient, whether it's meetings or morning routines. You don't play around when it comes to coffee—or life.

Your Strength: Quiet confidence with a hint of intensity.

Caramel Macchiato - The Life of the Party

You're bubbly, outgoing, and bring the good energy wherever you go. Your coffee order is sweet with a little drama—just like your flair for storytelling and TikTok reels. You thrive on connection and live for a good vibe.

Secret Ingredient: That caramel drizzle? Basically your aura.

Drip Coffee - The Classic

Reliable. Grounded. Timeless. You appreciate the little things—fresh air, good conversation, and hot coffee without all the bells and whistles. You probably read the paper (or at least skim it) and know how to enjoy the moment without distraction.

Best Paired With: A quiet corner seat and your favorite podcast.

So... what's your coffee personality?
Tag us on Instagram @taverncoffeehouse with your order and let us know if we nailed it. And if you're feeling bold, try something new next time—you might discover a side of yourself you haven't met yet.
