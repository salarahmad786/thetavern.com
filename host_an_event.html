Host an Event in Our Space

Need a welcoming space for your next meeting, study group, creative workshop, or small event?

At The Tavern Coffee House, we believe in creating more than just great coffee—we're here to build community. That's why we offer our cozy space for free to individuals and groups who need a place to gather, connect, or collaborate.

Whether you're hosting a local book club, planning a nonprofit meetup, or looking for a quiet space for conversation, The Tavern is proud to open our doors. While there's no rental fee, we do encourage a suggested donation of $550 to help us continue serving Buckeye through great brews, stronger jobs, and community-focused programs.

Every cup poured and every event hosted supports our mission to bring people together.
