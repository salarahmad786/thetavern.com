Why Morning People Have It Made (and How to Fake It...)

By Team Coffeehouse | COFFEE CULTURE • COFFEE LIFESTYLE

Spoiler: You don't need to be a zombie worshipper to enjoy early wins—just a good cup of coffee and a short-cut up your hoodie sleeve.

Let's face it: morning people seem to have life figured out. They've already three tasks deep before you're awake for the second time. Their skin glows. Their choice is sorted. Their coffee is already halfway gone.

If that's not you—it's okay. Some of us are simply built for slow mornings, long yawns, and questioning everything until we've had caffeine. But if you've ever wanted to fake being a morning person (or even become one), we've got you covered.

Here's how to channel your inner early bird—without giving up your night owl soul.

☀️ Why Morning People Always Seem One Step Ahead

  • They get more done with fewer distractions.
    Early hours are quiet, focused, and low-disruption. No emails, no notifications, no kids needing help with Wi-Fi. Just time to think.
  • They create space for calm, not chaos.
    Instead of rolling out of bed and straight into stress, morning people often move slowly and intentionally. That morning walk? That slow coffee moment? It sets the tone.
  • They enjoy the magic of first light.
    There's something grounding about watching the city wake up with you. And let's be real—sunrise morning photos looks pretty sweet through the Tavern's front windows.

☕ But What If You're Not a Morning Person? Here's How to Fake It.

We won't make you meditate at 5 AM or give up your Netflix binging. Here's a realistic plan:

1. Bribe Yourself with Coffee

Let your morning start with something to look forward to. Whether it's our Vanilla Latte, Cold Brew, or your favorite pastry from Humry Bumry's bakery, a custom-baked wake-up call makes it easier to fall out of bed.

Try this: Pre-order your drink and make the Tavern your first stop. You'll have a warm cup (and warm vibes) waiting for you.

2. Keep a "Bare Minimum" Routine

We're talking low-effort, high-payoff. Splash water on your face, make your bed, and sip your coffee without screens. Boom—you're officially functioning.
